# QA Findings: Arcforma Mail

Brand questions raised by this app. Everything here is a decision for a human, not a gap to fill quietly. Accepted findings are mirrored to `arcforma-brand/qa/FINDINGS.md`.

Status key: **OPEN** needs a decision. **NOTED** understood, no action needed. **RESOLVED** decided, with the decision recorded.

---

## F-MAIL-01: App icon is a placeholder, OPEN

The macOS app icon (`apps/desktop/build/icon.icns`) is a flat cobalt `#0845AC` rounded square generated by `apps/desktop/scripts/make-icon.mjs`. There is no approved standalone mark (brand finding F-02), so no glyph was cropped out of the wordmark and no mark was improvised.

**Decision needed:** export the checker glyph as a clean vector (F-02), then regenerate the icon from it. Until then the cobalt square ships as the icon.

---

## F-MAIL-02: brand.json has no desktop-app format, OPEN

`brand.json` lists formats for cards, social, OG, sting, and web pages. A desktop application surface (sidebar wordmark at 120 px on white, window chrome, list rows, reading pane) is not among them. The app applies the system by the rules in `DESIGN.md` (ink wordmark file on white, `--af-*` tokens only, no new hex, no shadows) and consumes the files verbatim through `scripts/sync-brand.mjs`.

**Decision needed:** add a `desktop-app` entry to `brand.json` naming the lockup (`arcforma-wordmark-ink.svg` at 120 px), the safe margins, and whether the wordmark and dither files may be used in an app surface. Until then the usage here is provisional.

## F-MAIL-03: floating panel shadow (Arcforma Text)
The system declares no shadow token. The selection toolbar and instruction panel float over other apps and need separation from the host window. Decision taken: NSPanel's default OS shadow, no custom value. Status: OPEN, needs brand sign-off.

## F-MAIL-04: toolbar narrower than the 120 px wordmark minimum (Arcforma Text)
The selection toolbar is about 90 px tall by 300 px wide; the wordmark's minimum width does not fit at a sane scale. Proposed: a Roboto Mono "TEXT TOOLS" eyebrow on the toolbar, wordmark only on the wider instruction panel. Status: OPEN, blocked on F-02 (standalone mark).

## F-MAIL-05: menu-bar glyph placeholder (Arcforma Text)
No approved standalone mark exists (brand F-02). The menu-bar item uses the SF Symbol `textformat` template image until the glyph is exported. Status: OPEN, blocked on F-02.

## F-MAIL-06: modal scrim and compose panel edge (Arcforma Mail), NOTED
Ask AI and Settings open as a card over the app; the compose panel floats over the reading pane. The system has no scrim or elevation token, so the scrim is a flat `--af-bg-alt` wash and every floating card is separated by a 1 px `--af-rule` edge, no shadow. Eyebrows for state (NO REPLY BY, BACK, SIGN IN TO CLAUDE CODE) use `.af-mono` in `--af-accent` because there is no state palette. Status: NOTED, no decision needed unless the brand adds elevation.

## F-MAIL-07: inbox-zero artwork is user-supplied, OPEN
The empty state of the inbox and of the Daily 0, Weekly 0, and Later queues shows a picture above the headline when `~/Library/Application Support/Arcforma Mail/inbox-zero.webp` (or `.png`, `.jpg`) exists. The main process serves it at `app://mail/user-art/inbox-zero`; without the file the empty state is text only. The brand system has no illustration or empty-state artwork, so this slot holds whatever Oliver drops in, at 420 px wide, 14 px radius, no shadow. Status: OPEN, pending a brand asset for the empty state.
