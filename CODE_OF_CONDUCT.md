# Code of conduct

Adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 3.0, available at https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/code_of_conduct/

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, and to act in ways that build an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behaviour

Show consideration and respect. Accept responsibility, apologise to those affected, and learn from mistakes. Focus on what is best for the community rather than for yourself alone. Assume that people are acting in good faith until you have real reason to think otherwise.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind. Trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks. Public or private harassment. Publishing another person's private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission. Any other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to conduct@arcforma.ai. All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The privacy and security of anyone who reports an incident will be respected.

## Addressing and repairing harm

The maintainer will follow these steps, choosing the one that fits the harm done.

1. **A private warning**, with an explanation of what was wrong and why, and where useful a request to apologise.
2. **Temporarily limited participation**, for example no interaction with the people involved for a stated period.
3. **A temporary suspension** from all participation in the project.
4. **A permanent ban**, for a sustained pattern of the above, or for harassment or aggression toward an individual or a group.

The aim at every step is to repair harm and to make continued participation possible where that is reasonable, not to punish.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces, and also when someone is representing the project in public.
